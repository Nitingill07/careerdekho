Ask on WhatsApp
Is Manipal University Jaipur approved for online degrees?

MUJ offers UGC-entitled online degree programs. Recognition should be verified for the specific program and academic intake before enrollment.

What is the fee for the MUJ Online MBA?

The current base program fee listed by Online Manipal is ₹1,80,000, or ₹45,000 per semester, before applicable scholarship, concession or financing terms.

How many MBA electives does MUJ offer?

Online Manipal currently lists 13 electives for the MUJ Online MBA. Learners may opt for one elective for a super specialization or two for a dual specialization, subject to current university rules.

Does MUJ Online MBA include free Coursera?

Current Online Manipal FAQ information says Coursera access can be opted for at a special student price rather than being automatically free. Confirm the latest terms before admission because bundled benefits can change.

Does Online Manipal provide placement support?

Online Manipal describes placement assistance, employability support, skill assessments, job opportunities and placement drives.

What scholarships or discounts are available?

MUJ lists scholarships for eligible categories and payment concessions that vary by program and payment method. These are subject to current terms, so check the payable amount for your intake before paying.
